To help students develop an intuitive understanding of **Wide-Sense Stationary (WSS)** random processes through the visualization of their **Autocorrelation functions** and **Power Spectral Densities (PSD)**. This experiment highlights the relationship between time-domain and frequency-domain representations of second-order statistics in random processes. Through the simulations we aim that students will learn to:

1. Distinguish between **stationary** and **wide-sense stationary** random processes.
2. Visualize the **autocorrelation function** as a function of time-lag (τ) in WSS processes.
3. Demonstrate how the **power spectral density** is obtained as the Fourier Transform of the autocorrelation function.
